Today, the Prime Minister has concluded an official visit to Central and Southern Malaysia. This report contains details on what happened during the visit.

## The more the merrier

---

For the first time, two other Plushie Kingdom citizens accompanied the Prime Minister during the official visit, being Brownie and Ah Chai. This demonstrates the Government's dedication to strengthening bonds within the community, with future visits potentially also allowing for more plushies to follow along.

## Journey to the South

---

![Picture showing the plushies having a snack](/assets/news-media/report-on-the-prime-ministers-visit-to-central-and-southern-malaysia/sg-perak.webp)

The visit began early at 5am, with the three plushies stopping at Sungai Perak for a quick rest and snack before moving on to the next location...

### Mitsui Outlet Park (KLIA)

![Picture showing Pink Bear Bear at Mitsui Outlet Park](/assets/news-media/report-on-the-prime-ministers-visit-to-central-and-southern-malaysia/mitsui.webp)

The plushies' first stop was Mitsui Outlet Park, a shopping mall located near Kuala Lumpur International Airport. In the mall, the plushies had a delicious lunch, while getting distracted with some shopping along the way!

## Melaka

---

### New arrivals

![Picture showing the plushies at the hotel in Melaka](/assets/news-media/report-on-the-prime-ministers-visit-to-central-and-southern-malaysia/melaka-hotel.webp)

The plushies reached Melaka, their first major destination around 5pm. While the Prime Minister was no stranger to hotel stays, Brownie and Ah Chai certainly appreciated staying somewhere different for once after spending many years in the Kingdom. The plushies rested in the hotel for a short while before beginning their exploration of the historical city...

### Jonker Street

This legendary shopping street was the plushies' first stop in Melaka. The various stalls and restaurants around the area provided them with a hearty meal, while the red buildings at Dutch Square proved to be great spots for taking photos!

### Encore Melaka

The next day, the plushies went to watch an entertaining theatre performance at the Encore Melaka venue, marvelling at the impressive stage design and effects, learning a lot about historical Melaka culture and taking some more photos along the way.

![Picture of Pink Bear Bear outside Encore Melaka](/assets/news-media/report-on-the-prime-ministers-visit-to-central-and-southern-malaysia/encore-outside.webp)
![Picture of Pink Bear Bear inside Encore Melaka](/assets/news-media/report-on-the-prime-ministers-visit-to-central-and-southern-malaysia/encore-inside.webp)

### Jonker Street (again)

On their second visit to Jonker Street, the plushies got to try out Melaka's unique chicken rice balls for dinner. After that, they also visited the Mamee House at Jonker Street, where they ended up bringing 2 more plushies with them!
