August has been a big month for pk-gov, headlined by the launch of PIBSS and Timeline, as well as bringing various fixes and improvements to the website! Today, that momentum continues with our newest set of updates to pk-gov which improve pk-gov's various content pages.

## Proper printing support

---

Although we strive to be a digital-first Government, it can be handy to have some of the pages from the website printed out for eye-friendly viewing! Previously, attempting to print pages would give you a broken layout with overlapping and unnecessary elements (the footer is useless in print since you can't click links!). Some design elements of the website such as the hero sections at the top of most pages would also waste large amounts of ink. Now, we've made an effort to ensure that pages on pk-gov look great in any medium, whether it's a screen or a piece of paper!

![comparison of print layouts before and after updates](/assets/news-media/print-pages-and-get-notified-with-the-latest-updates-to-pk-gov/print.webp)

## More ways to stay informed

---

The Government news page of pk-gov has been rapidly filled with increasing amounts of content, now containing 14 news articles! It can be hard to catch up with the latest happenings in the Kingdom, so we're introducing two new ways to stay informed, which can be accessed by clicking the new 'Manage preferences' button above the news articles...

![modal showing several options for getting Government news updates](/assets/news-media/print-pages-and-get-notified-with-the-latest-updates-to-pk-gov/notification-modal.webp)

### Push Notifications

As pk-gov is a website meant to help plushies get things done, most plushies don't regularly check the website. This has led to many users frequently missing various news articles, so we've decided to implement optional push notifications! Powered by <a class='link' href='https://onesignal.com/' target='_blank' rel='noreferrer'>OneSignal</a>, users who enable this feature will receive notifications whenever a new article is published, ensuring that users always stay up to date with the latest Government news!

### RSS Feed

Besides being notified, some plushies may prefer to read news articles with their favourite reader apps for a more familiar experience. To support this, we've integrated the Government news articles into an RSS feed. Users can copy the RSS feed link and add it to their favourite reader app, such as Feedly or Flipboard, to read new articles straight from the app!

## Moving forward

---

As always, we hope this latest set of updates will enhance your pk-gov experience even more. As pk-gov grows from a simple informational site to a provider of comprehensive information and services, we'll always make it our priority to make pk-gov the most intuitive, informative and effective Government resource it can be!
