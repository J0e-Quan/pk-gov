The process of gaining Plushie Kingdom citizenship is changing with the introduction of the Plushie Immigration and Biodata Storage Service (PIBSS). So, we've decided to guide new citizens through the new immigration process by filming a video!

## Developing ideas

---

After the clean "stage and presenter" setup of the PIBSS video event, we wanted to do something different for this video. As this would be shown to new plushies entering the Kingdom, the video was planned to be warm and welcoming, demonstrating the whole process in a short duration of time while giving enough information for plushies to fully understand the process.

## Filming the video

---

In the end, we decided to show the whole journey of a new plushie entering the Kingdom, seamlessly combining dialogue and interactions between the plushies with informative demonstrations and visual elements guiding viewers through the whole process. Kiwi and Kea were chosen to be the "main actors", as their contrast in personalities made the video much more entertaining! Being plushies from outside Malaysia, these two birds also demonstrated that the immigration process was simple and easy, no matter where you're from!

## The final result

---

A few days of filming and editing later, the Ministry of Immigration published a nearly 4-minute-long video, which you can watch on <a class='link' href='https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C9tG6MtXyI4' target='_blank' rel='noreferrer'>our YouTube channel</a>! We're very proud of the result, and hope that the video will help to explain the whole process of gaining Plushie Kingdom citizenship!
