Over the past few months, the Ministry of Technology has been tirelessly working to add several new features to pk-gov and improve the website's design and architecture for easier development in the future. As many of these changes were targeted towards the website's core functionality and internal files, we decided to release the new features all at once as a "big year end update", which is coming to pk-gov today!

## Preparing pk-gov for the future

---

### A leaner codebase

The complexity of a project's codebase can make a large difference in the speed at which new features can be delivered. To ensure that pk-gov can reliably improve and remain as the best Government resource available, we worked to make pk-gov's codebase as simple and efficient as possible, such as by reusing deisgn elements in several different pages and making the most out of the existing technologies used by pk-gov before choosing to introduce new ones.

### Standardised design

Since its launch, pk-gov has been the home of our bold and modern, yet warm and welcoming Government identity. However, design elements used in pk-gov such as spacing, font sizes and more had little to no consistency, relying on "eyeballing" values rather than detailed specifications. 

To make the design of pk-gov truly consistent, we have introduced new design tokens to the website. These are preset values for various design elements, removing the guesswork needed when designing new pages. Now, developers can simply choose from the design tokens available to style a page that always looks and feels like it belongs on pk-gov.

## Access developer information on pk-dev

---

In support of open access to information, we have added a new 'pk-dev' page which can be accessed from the footer. This is the home of developer information on pk-gov, giving you a closer look at how pk-gov is designed, structured and built! There are two sections in pk-dev which cater to different aspects of pk-gov:

### Design

This is where the Government identity guidelines page now resides, as well as where you can find a list of design tokens used throughout pk-gov and download a selection of Government assets.

### Code

This section dives into the technical details of pk-gov, such as what technologies the site uses and how files are organised. Examples of common UI components seen throughout pk-gov such as buttons and cards can also be found here!

## Get help when accessing invalid links

---

Whether its experimenting with URLs in the address bar or encountering invalid links on pk-gov, it is always possible for users to encounter what is known as a '404 error' when a page cannot be found. Previously, users would only be shown a "generic" error page with no advice on what to do next. So, we have added a new 404 error page that shows in these situations, aiming to give users clear guidance to help them find the content they were looking for!

## Peek behind the curtain with pk-gov-beta

---

One of our main priorities with pk-gov is to make it a reliable resource for accessing Government information and services. As a result, we only announce and release updates when they are 100% completed. However, curious users who want to try out new changes to pk-gov before they are released can now do so by visiting pk-gov-beta, the new 'beta' version of our website! As pk-gov-beta is always under development, it can be less stable than the existing pk-gov, so there are several visual cues indicating to users that they are on a different version of pk-gov:

- The 'pk-gov-beta' logo on the header
- The FAQ section in the homepage
- A section above the footer where users can report a bug or give feedback

This will enable us to announce upcoming features to pk-gov at an earlier date, as well as allowing users to try them out early.
