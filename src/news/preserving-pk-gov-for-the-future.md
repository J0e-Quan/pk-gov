Since pk-gov was first launched in May 2026, there has been countless new updates to the website, enhancing the website with various new features, bug fixes and services. Throughout all this change, we've regularly recorded the history of pk-gov in our <a class="link" href="/about/pk-gov/">About the pk-gov website</a> page, and the history of the Plushie Kingdom in the <a class="link" href="/info/who-we-are/">Who we are</a> page. This ensured that Plushie Kingdom history would be permanently stored for future viewing.

## Show, don't tell

---

Today, the Government is introducing an additional initiative to preserve the history of the pk-gov website. Although text descriptions of changes to the website will continue to be added as the website gets updated, we believe that it would be better for plushies to be able to "travel back in time" to view the history of pk-gov directly. So, all future updates to the <a class="link" href="https://archive.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">pk-gov Github repository</a> will now trigger a process of archiving the entire website into the <a class="link" href="https://github.com/J0e-Quan/pk-gov" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Internet Archive</a>.

## What is the Internet Archive?

---

The Internet Archive is a non-profit organisation that archives various forms of digital and physical media, including websites, books, and software. Archiving pk-gov on the Internet Archive ensures that all versions of pk-gov will be properly presevered for free!

## Using the pk-gov archives

---

### What is covered in the archives?

We plan to fully archive the entirety of the pk-gov website. This means all images, pages and content within the pk-gov website will be preserved. However, this does not neccessarily include content outside of pk-gov, such as YouTube videos or links to external websites.

### How to access the archives

To access the various archives of pk-gov, follow these steps:

1. Go to the <a class="link" href="https://archive.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Internet Archive</a> website
2. In the 'Wayback Machine' searchbar, enter 'pk-gov.onrender.com' and search
3. There will be a calender shown with dates and times where the website was archived (times shown are in UTC)
4. Choose any of the given timestamp links to view what pk-gov looked like at that point!
