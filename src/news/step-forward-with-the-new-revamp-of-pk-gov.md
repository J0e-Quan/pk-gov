As pk-gov expands from a simple informational site into a sprawling Government resource filled with helpful information and services, navigation of the website has become more difficult. Simple actions required scrolling and clicking through several layers of pages before finally landing on the desired page. Today, we're introducing another set of updates that aim to improve the navigation of pk-gov, among other improvements to the website.

## Revamped look

---

This update refreshes the appearance of several key elements in the website, giving them a new look while enhancing their functionality!

### Homepage

This update completely changes the look and layout of the homepage. We've moved services to their own section at the top with pages that provide information below it, followed by the ministry pages. This new layout puts content that is more frequently accessed near the top, speeding up navigation of the website. In fact, the revised layout means the 'Life in the Plushie Kingdom' page can be removed, saving an extra scroll and click for most interactions in pk-gov! The new cards that replace the old 'text and button' layout are also more compact while providing a much larger clickable area.

### Footer

The layout of links in the footer has also been updated to match the homepage's new layout. Now, most pages are directly accessible from links in the footer, making it more convenient to navigate pk-gov.

## Support for reduced motion

---

The Plushie Kingdom is home to plushies of various types, so it is important that pk-gov can be used by all plushies. We're proud to be taking the next step forward in making pk-gov more accessible, by introducing support for reduced motion. While the various animations in pk-gov aim to make content more visually appealing, these animations can cause discomfort for users with certain conditions. Now, users who have the relevant settings enabled on their device will see a version of pk-gov that is free of movement and animations. This helps to prevent problems such as nausea and dizziness when using the website, ensuring pk-gov remains intuitive, informative and effective no matter what type of plushie uses it!

## Dynamic Bad Guy outfits

---

![all bad guy outfits](/assets/news-media/step-forward-with-the-new-revamp-of-pk-gov/bad-guys.webp)

Now that major initiatives like PIBSS have been launched, it is time to address the biggest complaint about the website since it's initial launch, being that the sketch of the Bad Guy did not accurately reflect his attire at all times. To address this, the Ministry of Defence went on a large-scale spying operation, gathering valuable data on the Bad Guy's favourite outfits. 

Today, that data will finally be utilised to dynamically change the outfit of the Bad Guy's sketch in the 'Safety and Residency' page! We pieced together various outfits that were more likely to be worn by the Bad Guy, so that plushies can be more aware of the Bad Guy's presence. 

## Taking the next step forward

---

We hope navigating pk-gov will become faster and easier with these new updates to pk-gov! We're proud of the consistent stream of updates improving the website, and we will always take the next step forward to make pk-gov the most intuitive, informative and effective Government resource it can be!
